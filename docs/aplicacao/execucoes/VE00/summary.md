# VE00 — Summary

VE00 (Reconciled baseline) is **verified** for its recorded scope: the web app and its published data, with native and device checks explicitly not run. The app's core journey works on web in all five pilot areas with real cadastral buildings. I drove link → listing text → building suggestion → confirmation → dossier (Destaques, Entorno, Edifício) → visit → comparison → reload for Boa Viagem, Casa Forte, Graças, Espinheiro and Madalena. The building was suggested automatically 5/5 times, and saved analyses and links survived a reload ([baseline.md](baseline.md), [pilot journey](scripts/ve00-pilot-journey.mjs)). I reused the existing v1 web smoke and test suite, and added three reproducible scripts. [reconcile_baseline.py](scripts/reconcile_baseline.py) recomputes every plan §2 figure from the active publication `indice-municipal/91b71d239c1847cf` with saved hashes ([coverage.json](coverage.json)). [gtfs_daily_count_check.py](scripts/gtfs_daily_count_check.py) quantifies the transit-count defect. [select_eval_sets.py](scripts/select_eval_sets.py) creates the first evaluation sets: G-BUILDING has 50 real buildings with a street-grouped dev/test split, and G-COMPARE has 20 rent/purchase pairs with clearly synthetic listing values ([registry](../../evaluation-sets/README.md)). No tracked file, user change or notebook was modified; the 39 pre-existing dirty files, including the deleted root `index.html`, are recorded rather than touched. Nothing new reaches customers in this epoch. What it delivers is a trustworthy map of what each screen shows, where each number comes from, and which numbers are wrong ([issues.md](issues.md)).

Checks run: typecheck (0 errors), lint (0 errors, 5 warnings), Jest (53/53 suites, 1,414/1,414 tests), the v1 web smoke (50 audited steps at 375 and 1,280 px, 0 findings, 0 external requests) and the new five-area journey (5/5 areas, 0 page or console errors, 0 px overflow at 375 px). Data checks: all 72 partitions match their manifest checksums and counts. Plan figures were confirmed: 7,206 buildings, 2,688 in the pilot, 2,622 centroids within 50 m of their own street, 96.5% within 300 m of a CNEFE point, and 630 ITBI-gated buildings. One figure is corrected: the 2022 Defesa Civil cluster is 12 rows on one date plus 2 two days earlier, not 14 on one date. Three defects are newly measured. First, the dossier's customer-facing "paradas diárias no GTFS" adds up weekday, Saturday and Sunday timetables. That gives a median of 2.42 times the weekday count, and 146 of the selected "corridor" stops have no weekday service. Second, the 2020 and 2022 Defesa Civil files are shifted by one column. Third, two different transit feed versions are in use, and the published layer records neither. My own errors were fixed during the run: an invalid test wallet seed (which the app correctly rejected), a case-sensitive text probe, a wrong post-reload wait, and early parser mistakes in the reconciliation script. All evidence is automated, from a desktop web build. There was no field observation, no native device run, no target-device performance measurement and no participant study. Customer usefulness rests only on an expert walkthrough: Compare offers no decision brief, and its first conclusion falls below the fold on a phone.

Decision: **adopt** the baseline, scripts and evaluation sets as the reference for later epochs. No VE00 work remains. Native and device verification stay open for VE12, or earlier if a device becomes available. The next eligible epoch is **VE01 (useful comparison)**, and VE02 is also eligible. The first concrete task is VE01.01: audit `custos.ts::custoMensal`, `comparar.ts::compararCusto/compararEspaco/compararEntorno` and `dossie/derivar.ts`. From those, define the single comparison projection that dossier and Compare will share, label the expected outcome of each G-COMPARE case, and deliberately revise the legacy wording tests listed in baseline §5, keeping the bans on pejorative and safety wording. The quick wording fix for the overstated transit count (issue I01) should land in VE01 or VE02. The calendar-correct replacement belongs to VE05. VE01 needs no external dependency. Its customer-validation step needs real people currently searching for a home, and until they are available it stays pending without blocking the engineering work.
