# VE04 — Summary

VE04 (Essentials and an independent plan B) is **partial**: everything is implemented and the automated acceptance checks pass, but no person has reviewed the venue sample and the completeness of the mapped venues is unknown. Pilot buildings with a strong entrance link now get a dossier section, "A pé, a partir da entrada" ([DiaADia.tsx](../../../../src/componentes/dossie-v1/DiaADia.tsx)), listing the nearest mapped grocery, pharmacy, square or park and bus or metro stop with walking minutes along the streets; when the route crosses a bridge, the section names it once and every row gives a plan B that avoids it, and where it saves a meaningful distance it shows one combined grocery-and-pharmacy trip instead of two. Buildings with a weak link get only a straight-line distance with no walking claim. The Compare brief now names a material daily-life difference ("Graças fica a 1 min a pé de uma farmácia; Casa Forte, a 14 min") and, when cost and space do not separate two homes, uses it to say which to visit first. Underneath sit a 7,272-venue catalogue ([catalogo.mjs](../../../../scripts/dados/destinos/catalogo.mjs)) built from the archived OSM extract, Prefeitura records (street fairs with days and hours, public markets, municipal pharmacies), official squares and the pinned GTFS feed, with duplicates merged, closed venues excluded and same-chain branches kept apart; a reachability engine ([alcance.mjs](../../../../scripts/dados/destinos/alcance.mjs)) on the published VE03 graph; a validated per-area app payload of 1.11 MB; and the G-POI review sheet. No new input or onboarding is required ([evaluation](evaluation.md)).

Checks run: Node tests 60/60, covering duplicates, a same-chain distinct venue, closed venues, missing hours, unsuitable entrances, a shared bottleneck (the naive second venue shares the bridge, the plan B does not) and errand chains; Jest 57/57 suites and 1,480 tests (10 new) with G-COMPARE still 20/20; typecheck and lint clean; v1 web smoke 50/50; rendered checks at 375 and 360 px with no overflow, page errors or external requests. On real data, most strong-link buildings reach a grocery and a pharmacy within 10 minutes on foot, from 1,404 and 1,295 of 1,761 in Boa Viagem to all 151 in Espinheiro, with Casa Forte lowest at 57 and 46 of 78. In Boa Viagem 335 grocery routes cross a bridge and for 188 of them the naive second-nearest grocery depends on the same bridge, while an independent plan B was always found within 2 km. Of 2,514 errand chains none was longer than separate trips (median saving 357–504 m), and the G-ROUTE cross-check found 0 violations across 9 routes, with 4 network-nearest stops closer than the straight-line choice. The first renders exposed four product defects, all fixed: a headline that contradicted the walking difference, a bridge named three times, stops shown as numeric codes, and a payload version that ignored format changes; a stale dev-server bundle also needed a restart. The main caveat is that the pilot has only 48 mapped groceries and 53 mapped pharmacies, so distances are to the nearest *mapped* venue, which the dossier states in one line, and G-POI accuracy is unmeasured. Cost was about 100 s and 1.2 GB per run, 3 public API requests and no spend; all evidence is automated, from a desktop web build, with no customer data.

Decision: **iterate**. The remaining work is to have a person review the 44-venue [G-POI sheet](../../evaluation-sets/g-poi-revisao.csv) and the VE03 sheets, add missing venues through a venue corrections file that does not exist yet, measure catalogue completeness for at least one neighbourhood, interpret opening hours beyond raw text, and check the payload size on a low-end device. The next eligible epoch is **VE05 (scheduled routine by hour)**: VE02 is verified, VE03 and VE04 supply the network and the destination catalogue, and its first task, VE05.01, is to resolve the pinned GTFS v2026.10.26 feed by service date (calendars and exceptions, after-midnight times, directions) and replace the summed-calendar corridor counts that VE00 found and VE01 hid. Walking and transit claims stay unverified until a person reviews the VE03 and VE04 samples.
