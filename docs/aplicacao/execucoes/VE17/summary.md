# VE17 — Summary

VE17 closed the programme with an audit instead of new features. All forty applications have an individual destination with an evidence link and a trigger:
- 6 implemented and engineering-verified (decision brief, cost trade-off, decisive question, evidence compiler, routine by hour, update operations);
- 1 experiment passed (Torre expansion);
- 2 rejected (shaded routes, flood model);
- 20 partial;
- 9 deferred with a named trigger;
- 2 retained.

The grouped rows are split. All 217 research papers have their own row, mapped to 19 families: 43 reference papers are kept as reference, and papers whose method waits for data stay deferred instead of disappearing. A feature register gives every enabled screen block its data, update policy and owner role. A next-programme document says what to keep, merge, simplify, expand and stop.

The longitudinal and data-advantage measures were computed from what exists, not assumed. Across 2,825 buildings in six areas, 2,643 (93.6%) get at least three results that exist only because entrances, the walking network, places, timetables and event records are kept linked. On average, 70.5% of the per-building blocks shown depend on those maintained links rather than a single copyable lookup. The advantage held in a later period (2022–23 history still ranked 2024 floods best) and in a new area (Torre's link quality matches the pilot's). Reuse is real and concentrated: four linked flood records drive every rain consequence in the app, one of them 147 dossiers. One correction can therefore help many homes, but one wrong link can mislead them. None of the four has been reviewed.

VE17 is honestly partial, because two kinds of evidence the plan expects do not exist yet:
- **Customer outcomes:** no participant sessions, and no telemetry by design.
- **Human review:** 0 corrections and 0 review minutes, with 259 rows waiting.

Owners are roles, not people, because none has been assigned. The next programme turns these gaps into its first job, with three hypotheses and stop rules:
1. Review the four rain links (hide the block if fewer than three hold).
2. Run five paired participant sessions on whether the comparison speeds a justified visit choice.
3. Time one review sheet; expand to Parnamirim or Aflitos only if review costs at most a minute per row.

The budget is R$ 0, six review hours, five sessions and two devices. With VE17 recorded, the VE00–VE17 plan has no further epoch; the next eligible work is that programme.
