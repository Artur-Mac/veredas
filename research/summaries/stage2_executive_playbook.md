## 2. Core Discoveries & Validated Evidence

### A. The Portal Moral Hazard Confirmed (Zillow / CRMLS Case)
In late 2025, Zillow removed the direct, on-page display of First Street climate risk scores following massive pushback from real estate agents and the California Regional MLS (CRMLS), who complained that color-coded risk badges were "spooking" buyers and causing cancellations. This proves that **listing portals economically cannot display dealbreakers** without cannibalizing broker ad revenues. An independent consumer advocate has an unassailable moral hazard moat.

### B. Post-Mortems of Dead Startups
* **Localize.city ($70M Raised; Shuttered US Aug 2024)**: Proved that B2C consumers refuse to pay monthly subscriptions for passive neighborhood data during infrequent transactions, while trying to monetize via broker leads created fatal conflicts of interest.
* **Zillow Offers ($500M+ Loss; 1,200 Layoffs)**: Proved that statistical AVMs fail completely at pricing real estate without unit-level physical and maintenance inspection ground-truth.
* **Loft (Brazil; 1,200 Layoffs 2022-2023)**: Proved that holding capital-heavy property inventory in Latin America is fatal during interest-rate hikes; survived only by pivoting to financial services (CredPago) and SaaS (Vista).

### C. The 50 Compound Interaction Signals
A single data point is fragile. The core breakthrough lies in **interaction effects**:
1. *Traffic + Narrow Street Canyon + West Facade* = Acoustic reflection echo chamber + thermal heat trap.
2. *Spring Tide (>2.2m) + Cloudburst + Low Elevation* = Tide-locked storm sewer backflow in coastal deltas (Recife).
3. *Low Condo Fee + Building Age > 25 Years + Multiple Elevators* = Imminent $10k+ special assessment (*cota extra*) time-bomb.
4. *Days on Market > 180d + 3 Price Cuts + Distress Keywords* = Aggressive below-ask negotiation leverage (-10% to -15%).
