## 1. Context & Motivation

Choosing where to live is one of the highest-stakes financial and personal decisions in a person's life, especially in dense Brazilian metropolitan centers like São Paulo and Recife. 

Today, home seekers are forced to make decisions based almost entirely on real estate advertisements designed for marketing conversion:
* Listings regularly omit exact addresses and building names to protect broker exclusivity.
* True monthly housing outlays (remote doorman fees, maintenance assessments, garbage taxes, insurance) are concealed behind sanitized rent and condo estimates.
* Crucial urban context—such as severe pedestrian slopes, recurrent street waterlogging, or imminent 2-year construction projects on adjacent parcels—is excluded.

---

## 2. The Solution: VEREDA

VEREDA is a mobile-first decision intelligence companion that ingests a listing URL and generates a comprehensive, verifiable **Decision Dossier** in 60 seconds:
1. **Building & Parcel Resolution:** Correlates advertised descriptors with official municipal datasets (GeoSampa, CNEFE/IBGE, OpenStreetMap).
2. **Audited Monthly Cost:** Dissects declared charges vs. actual mandatory fees.
3. **Traceable Evidence:** Replaces marketing hype with cited municipal records, environmental alerts, and walking route geometries.
4. **Actionable Unknowns:** Converts unverified records into physical field-test checklists and pre-formatted WhatsApp inquiries for the broker.
5. **Trade-Off Synthesis:** Enables side-by-side comparison of candidate homes focused on human trade-offs rather than arbitrary 0–10 scores.
